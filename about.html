AN OPEN SHELF, FOR EVERYONE

A place for good finds.

Common Room is an independent digital collection for films, games, books, and music that can be shared legally.

A small, honest beginning

This starter catalogue contains clearly labelled fictional demo entries. The Quiet Earth is an original twelve-second motion study, Signal Garden is a working browser game, Field Notes is an original short text, and Still Rooms contains original synthesized audio. Other entries demonstrate the catalogue and do not offer invented downloads. Names are fictional and do not refer to commercial works with similar titles.

Source & Licence

The original demo text, generated audio, motion study, and Signal Garden source are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 to the extent permitted by law. Catalogue artwork uses landscape photographs under the Unsplash Licence; these photographs are not part of the CC0 dedication and are not offered as standalone downloads. See artwork provenance.

Every item includes its own source and licence. Free access alone is not permission to redistribute. Public-domain status can vary by country. We do not claim ownership over third-party works.

Your privacy

Saved titles and reading positions stay in this browser. No analytics, advertising pixels, or accounts. Clearing browser storage removes your library. Anonymous discussions store your chosen display name, comment, and timestamp. A short-lived salted network hash limits spam; raw IP addresses are not stored in D1. Hosting providers may retain operational logs.

Corrections & takedown requests

This is an undeployed demonstration. A public licensing contact has not yet been configured. The operator must provide a monitored contact address before public launch.

When reporting a licence issue, include the item link, the affected material, evidence of the rights involved, and a way to contact you. The operator should temporarily remove disputed downloads while reviewing the claim.

Discussion guidelines

Talk about the work. Be considerate. Do not post personal information, spam, threats, or unlawful content. Comments are reviewed before appearing. Display names are not verified identities.
